## 🔧 Troubleshooting

**No aimbot when executing?**
- Check that you attached to the correct Roblox process (RobloxPlayerBeta.exe)
- Make sure you executed after the game world loaded
- Hit `F8` to toggle it on — it might be off by default

**Crashes happen?**
- Reboot your PC and try again
- Lower graphics settings in Roblox
- Close background apps (Chrome, Discord, etc.)

**Aim is laggy?**
- Reduce sensitivity in settings
- Close other programs running on your PC
- Update your graphics drivers
